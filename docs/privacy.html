Privacy Policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

PDF Studio collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores nothing outside your own device. There is no account, no server and no analytics.

What we collect

Nothing. Not usage data, not crash reports, not advertising or device identifiers, not your documents, not even their names.

This is not only a promise. The app is sandboxed without the network entitlement, so it has no ability to transmit anything — the capability is absent and the operating system enforces that. You can verify it: inspect the app's entitlements, or turn off Wi-Fi and mobile data and watch every feature keep working.

Your documents

Your PDFs stay where you put them: on your device, or in whichever storage provider you opened them from — iCloud Drive, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive or another. The app reads and writes them through Apple's document system, with your permission, and touches nothing you have not opened.

Everything the app does happens locally, including the work that other apps send to a server:

  • Converting to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text or images
  • Text recognition (OCR) on scans
  • Editing text, redaction and compression

Camera and photos

If you scan a document, the camera captures it and the scan becomes a PDF on your device. If you import photos, only the images you pick are read. Neither is uploaded anywhere. You can decline both permissions and the rest of the app works normally.

Purchases

The Pro subscription is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Whether the subscription is active is verified on your device from a record the App Store signed — there is no entitlement server, so we do not know who subscribes and could not tell you if asked.

Apple's own data collection

Apple may collect information about App Store downloads and purchases under Apple's privacy policy. That is between you and Apple; we receive only anonymous, aggregated sales totals that identify nobody.

What the App Store shows

The app's App Store listing reports Data Not Collected. Every category — contact info, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, user content — is answered "not collected", because none of it is.

Third-party code

None. The app has no dependencies outside Apple's own frameworks, so there is no partner collecting data on our behalf — the usual reason an otherwise private app has to answer "yes" on a privacy label.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear here with a new date. Since the app collects nothing today, a change would most likely mean a new capability was added — and it would be described plainly rather than buried.
