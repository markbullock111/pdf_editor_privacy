A PDF editor that never sends your documents anywhere

Most PDF apps upload your file to a server to convert or edit it. This one has no code that uploads anything — every edit, conversion and text recognition runs on your own device.

Your contracts, medical records and financial statements stay where you put them. Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account.

What it does

  • Edits the real text inside a page — not a text box pasted on top — keeping the original size, weight and style.
  • Redacts properly. Characters are deleted from the page and pixels erased from scanned images, rather than covered with a black box.
  • Converts to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, text and images without uploading anything.
  • Annotates and signs — highlight, draw, add notes, shapes and stamps; sign with a finger or Apple Pencil.
  • Organises pages — reorder, rotate, merge, split, extract, add page numbers and watermarks.
  • Reads comfortably — Day, Night and Sepia modes, read aloud, and a full-screen presentation mode.
  • Scans and recognises text on device, so a scan becomes searchable.

Free and Pro

Reading, searching, navigating and sharing are free and always will be — the free tier is a genuinely useful PDF reader, not a demo. PDF Studio Pro unlocks editing, conversion, redaction, signing and text recognition for US$9.99 per year, and can be shared with your family group. It renews automatically until you cancel it, which you can do at any time in Settings. No account is needed either way — see the terms.
